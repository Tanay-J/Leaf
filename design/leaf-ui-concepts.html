IV. Islands

Across the archipelago, the tide advanced through the mangrove roots with a quiet, clockwork persistence. The islands remembered tectonic grief long after maps forgot their names.

Girija Prasad stood by the veranda rail with his field notebook open, listening to the rain gather over the ridge.

In the margins of his journal, he sketched the outline of the reef and marked the exact hour the barometer dropped.

“You can measure the salinity of a creek,” Chanda Devi had told him once, “but not the memory of the monsoon that fed it.”

How Browsers Schedule Rendering & Frame Budgets

Every 60Hz display gives the browser roughly 16.6 milliseconds to run input handlers, execute JavaScript, recalculate styles, layout geometry, paint layers, and composite the frame.

When long tasks monopolize the main thread, input events queue behind layout work and the user experiences perceptible jank. Yielding control back to the event loop lets high-priority rendering flush on time.
